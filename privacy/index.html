Website privacy

Visits, without profiles.

Little Brush Games operates the Andy & Nata website. On andynata.com, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page views, visits, referral sources and page performance.

Cloudflare says this service does not use analytics cookies, local storage or fingerprinting to recognise people across visits. It provides aggregate reports rather than individual visitor profiles. This website no longer loads Google Analytics.

Page addresses, referring pages, browser and device information, approximate country and performance measurements are processed to produce these reports. Cloudflare also handles network requests to deliver and protect the website; cookie-free analytics does not mean that hosting processes no connection data.

Read Cloudflare Web Analytics documentation and Cloudflare's privacy policy for details. The Little Brush Games mirror does not load this website analytics service.

This notice supplements our company and game privacy policy. For privacy questions, contact hello@littlebrushgames.com.
