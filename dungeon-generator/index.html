Sketch
& Build.

A procedural tool for Unity

Turn simple shapes into places to explore. Sketch & Build is a procedural tool for Unity that we're developing around rooms, walls, bridges, and decoration — shaped with polygons and splines.

Explore the tool ↓

Built together by Andy & Nata.

What we're building

Shape it. Build it. Make it yours.

An early look at the workflow. Features are still evolving as development continues.

  • Room generation from simple shapes
  • Procedural wall generation
  • Bridges shaped along splines
  • Decoration layers for placing objects
  • Editable polygons and splines
  • Graph-based generation workflows in progress

Still on the workbench.

Sketch & Build is unfinished and planned for the Unity Asset Store. We'll share more as the tool takes shape; release details are not announced yet.
