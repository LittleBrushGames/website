A procedural tool for Unity

From the shape of a room to the path through a dungeon. We're building a tool to turn layouts into editable spaces — with more room to experiment.

Explore the tool ↓

Built together by Andy & Nata.

What we're building

Shape it. Build it. Make it yours.

A procedural workflow that leaves room for hands-on editing.

  • Editable spline and polygon sources
  • Room generation and composition
  • Walls, floors, and stairs
  • Stone and wooden bridges
  • Deterministic object scatter
  • Path overlays and diagnostics

Still on the workbench.

Planned for the Unity Asset Store. Release details will follow.
“Dungeon Generator” is a working description, not the final product name.
