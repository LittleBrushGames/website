Good to know.

What is Truth or Mole?

A local social-deduction party game where most players answer truthfully, while one or more hidden Moles must bluff and survive the group vote.

Does everyone need the app?

No. The whole group shares one Android phone or tablet and passes it from player to player.

Does the Mole get a fake question?

No. Every player gets a different public prompt from the selected topic. The Mole lies when answering their own assigned prompt.

How many people can play?

Truth or Mole supports 3–16 players on one device.

Which languages are included?

English, German, and Russian are available in the current Android release.

Does the game require an account?

No Little Brush Games account or room code is required.
