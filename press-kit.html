Short description

Truth Or Mole is a quick pass-and-play party game: pass one phone, answer funny questions, and find the secret Mole before the reveal.

How it works

Pick a pack, pass the phone, answer questions, bluff if you are the Mole, vote, then reveal who fooled the table.
