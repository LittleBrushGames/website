How to play

It should take longer to accuse someone than to learn the rules.

Choose a pack, pass the phone, answer prompts, accuse loudly, and reveal who was bluffing.

1

Choose

Pick a pack: family-friendly, friends night, or adults-only.

2

One phone goes around the table. No extra installs for everyone.

3

Answer

Most players tell the truth. The Mole quietly gets creative.

4

Accuse

Read the room, interrogate friends, and enjoy the suspicious confidence.

5

Reveal

Vote, reveal the Mole, celebrate or immediately demand a rematch.

FAQ

Still suspicious?

Quick answers for players, parents, reviewers, and suspicious friends.

Do all players need the app?

No. Truth Or Mole is designed around one shared phone passed around the table.

Is it family-friendly?

It can be. Family-friendly and adults-only content should be separated by pack selection.

Will there be ads?

No ads are planned. The current direction is a clean party-game experience.

Where can I get it?

iOS and Android are planned. For now, contact Little Brush Games for updates.
