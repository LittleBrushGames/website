Before the first accusation

Good to know.

Is Truth or Mole available now?

Yes. The Android version is live on Google Play. An iOS version is not currently available.

Does everyone need the app?

No. The whole group shares one phone and passes it from player to player.

How many people can play?

Truth or Mole supports 3–16 players on one device.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No Little Brush Games account is required. Google Play may require a connection for installation, purchases, and some platform services.

Which languages are included?

English, German, and Russian are available in the current game.

Where can I request support?

Email support@littlebrushgames.com.
